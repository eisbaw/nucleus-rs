paper-correctness S1 (MISLEADING). Sites: chapters/06-architecture.tex:181-186 and 198-205; chapters/07b-compilation-walkthrough.tex:243-251; chapters/08-validation.tex:159-168. The paper frames the conflict-freedom CHECK as confirming the precondition that licenses single-order replay for boundedness/deadlock. But in nucleus-compiler/src/passes/net_soundness.rs, check_conflict_free is a complete coverability BFS only for nets <=64 transitions (CONFLICT_BFS_TRANSITION_LIMIT); for the large multi-worker nets the bit-identity results actually rest on (e.g. 16-jacobi/distributed x mp-tcp-event at ~522 transitions / 24 contested places) it falls back to a single-order under-approximation that may miss a conflict reachable only on an off-order interleaving. What actually makes the replay sound on those nets is a STRUCTURAL construction: acfg_to_net per-worker control-place threading serialises every worker so a free-choice conflict is impossible by construction (net_soundness.rs:282-289,444-476,584-592). Fix: state conflict-freedom holds by construction for emitted nets, and describe the in-gate check as a regression tripwire (complete for small nets, sound never-false-rejecting under-approximation for large ones). Do not credit the check with verifying a precondition it does not fully verify on the load-bearing nets.
