## Background

TASK-0335 cycle 158 architect review P2.1: `inject_in_sequence`'s hoisted-Waits-drain dedup at transfer_inject.rs lines 1208-1218 uses `out.iter().any(matches!(...))` across the entire `out` Vec on the same (role, src, dst, data, tile) 4-tuple key the cycle-158 fix now respects with Sync-stopping, BUT this site does NOT stop at Sync. Per the cycle-158 narrative, 'duplicate Wait across a Sync barrier is legitimate (different buffer place)'.

## Risk

If a future schedule hoists a Wait past a Sync into a Sequence already containing a matching same-tile Wait in a prior epoch, this site will silently suppress the hoisted Wait and the consumer in epoch-2 will deadlock (its buffer place never filled because the producer's epoch-2 Push went to a different buffer; epoch-1's already-collapsed buffer place doesn't fire for epoch-2).

## In-tree status (cycle 158)

Currently LATENT — no in-tree schedule hoists a Wait across a Sync into a Sequence with a matching prior-epoch Wait. Pass A (`hoist_invariant_waits`) operates on the output of `inject_in_sequence` which the cycle-158 fix has already epoch-collapsed; the hoist's input is already 'clean' for the in-tree shapes.

## Cycle 159 — slot-aware Sync-stopping helper (AC#1 + AC#2 + AC#3 complete)

### AC#1 (empirical investigation)
LATENT confirmed in-tree at cycle 158/159. No schedule produces two block-inner sibling Repeats separated by a Sync that share a matching hoist-drain Wait key. Verified by running the existing 559-test dev suite + 906-test release suite + e2e 112/99/0/13/0 BEFORE applying the widening — all green. The dedup at transfer_inject.rs (line ~1208 helper call, comment block opening) was never observed to fire in the cross-Sync arm on the in-tree corpus.

### AC#2 (remediation chosen: NEW HELPER, not parameter)
**Initial mis-choice (corrected mid-cycle):** orchestrator first widened the call site to use cycle-158's `is_duplicate_xfer_in_epoch` directly. Re-trace showed the cycle-158 helper is the WRONG SHAPE for this site — it scans tail-anchored backward from end(out), but the drain at this site inserts AT `slot` (often far from tail). With a sibling drain placing the LATER-slot Wait first (reverse-slot iteration), the cycle-158 helper would still over-suppress just by finding a different sibling on the wrong side of the Sync.

**Final choice:** introduced a new helper `is_duplicate_xfer_in_epoch_at_slot(out, slot, cand)` that scans bidirectionally from `slot`, both directions stopping at the first ACFGNode::Sync. Substituted at transfer_inject.rs in the hoisted-Waits-drain dedup. New-fn-vs-Option<usize>-param rationale captured in the helper docstring (cycle-159 architect P3.1 fold-back): shape is fundamentally different (bidirectional vs tail-anchored), not a scope refinement.

### AC#3 (regression test)

### Gotchas & subtleties (forward-carried lessons)

- **The cycle-158 helper is NOT a universal solution.** Its tail-anchored backward scan assumes the candidate is appended at the END of `out`. For sites that insert at arbitrary `slot` (`out.insert(slot, ...)`), it can still over-suppress by finding a sibling-drain's match on the wrong side of an interior Sync. The slot-aware sibling helper is required when the insertion point is interior.
- **'Latent' does not imply 'no defensive value'.** The slot-aware fix doesn't fix any in-tree-observed deadlock today, but it changes the failure mode of a future trip from silent-deadlock to silent-correctness (both Waits preserved, both Petri buffer places filled). The cost is small: a few-LoC bidirectional helper + one regression test.
- **Mechanism-vs-bite separation in test docstrings.** Initial test docstring claimed the outer Repeat was load-bearing because place_or_bubble would re-collapse the Waits at the top scope. Architect P2.2 caught this: the Slot::Wait arm of hoist_invariant_waits pushes drained Waits directly (line ~1520), bypassing place_or_bubble. The test still BITES, but for a different reason than the docstring claimed — a recurrence of `feedback-implementer-disclosure-mechanism-wrong` from MEMORY. Fixed: docstring now correctly describes the Slot::Wait path.
