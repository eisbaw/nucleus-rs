=== Forward-carried from TASK-0049.04 slice-A review (architect P2 — bin slice MUST handle) ===
Two latent transport-correctness traps that are INERT against the compile-only StubShim (so slice A correctly left them) but BITE under a real UART-hub shim:
1. DMA channel-id namespace COLLISION. The effectful load/save Fires hardcode chan=0 (embedded-pattern lib.rs ~:1021/1024/1051/1054) while cross-worker Push/Wait derive chan from SeqTag. In the generated host run(), channel 0 simultaneously carries load_input dma_wait(0), the Push of seq-0 data, AND save_output dma_push(0)+dma_wait(0) — three unrelated transfers on one channel. Against the no-op StubShim this is inert + compiles; under a real shim it is NOT a faithful encoding. The bin slice needs a channel ALLOCATOR or a disjoint namespace separating effectful-IO channels from cross-worker-transport channels.
2. SyncTag -> u32 TRUNCATION. The lowering emits `shim.irq_barrier({tag} as u32)` casting a u64 SyncTag to u32. Harmless for small monotonic tags today; a silent truncation surface for the bin/real shim — widen the shim tag type or guarantee the tag range.
Also carry the slice-A receive-local mechanism (Wait declares a zero-init fixed-[T;N] local then dma_wait; real shim must actually fill it via RX) + the TASK-0049.01 receiver-RX-enabled-before-sender-TX start-gating discipline the generated .resc MUST emit.
