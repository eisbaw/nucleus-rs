## What this exercises

- partition=rows on M (output rows) — each worker owns a contiguous row band of `y[]` and the corresponding rows of `val[]` and `col_idx[]`. Same shape as 08-histogram/distributed and 03-reduction/distributed (well-trodden territory).
- transfer_inject for the dense input vector `x[N]`: every worker needs the FULL `x` (each row's nonzeros may reference any column). This is host-broadcast of x to all workers — same shape as 07-matmul's a-row broadcast under partition=rows.
- No cross-row dependency on `y` — each worker writes a disjoint band of y[i]. No accumulator fan-in needed.
