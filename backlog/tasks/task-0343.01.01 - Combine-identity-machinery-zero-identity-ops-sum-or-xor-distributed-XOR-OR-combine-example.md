## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Land the first, low-regression slice of the combine-identity generalization (parent TASK-0343.01). Today the overlapping-write accumulator fan-in combine is HARDCODED to wrapping_add (sum) in backend-common multi_worker_walker/wait.rs::render_accumulate_assign / accumulate_op_for_scalar. This slice makes the combine identity DECLARED by a kernel attribute and threads it to codegen for the THREE algebraic identities that share additive-identity ZERO (sum, or, xor) — so the existing zero-init (render_array_init_for / rust_scalar_zero, duplicated in backend-common render/types.rs + openmp-rs + mpi_plan + pthreads-sync) needs NO change. The non-zero-identity ops (min->MAX, max->MIN, and->all-ones) require identity-aware init across those ~4 sites and are DEFERRED to TASK-0343.01.02.

NEW codegen/IR surface (the depth this exercises): a 'combine=<op>' kernel attribute end-to-end (grammar + AST + parser + sidecar) plus NEW emitted code — the or arm emits 'name[_k] = name[_k] | _tmp[_k];' and xor 'name[_k] = name[_k] ^ _tmp[_k];' (operator form), distinct from sum's '.wrapping_add(...)' method form. Plus a SOUNDNESS gate: make the accumulate-fan-in reject STRICT — an accumulate fan-in whose owning kernel declares no combine identity is EmitError (today the sum-only carve-out silently assumes sum).

GRAMMAR GOTCHA (verified 2026-06-05): 'combine' is ALREADY a kernel identifier in 03-reduction and 23-dot-product. The attribute keyword MUST be contextual (positional, after purity) and MUST NOT be added to algo/tokens.rs reserved words, or those examples break. Kernel-name position (after 'kernel') and attribute position (after purity, before ';') never overlap, so contextual parse is unambiguous.

DETt-deterministic note: or and xor are associative+commutative -> order-independent across worker arrival -> bit-identical (PRD 10.1), strengthening the determinism story vs float sum.
