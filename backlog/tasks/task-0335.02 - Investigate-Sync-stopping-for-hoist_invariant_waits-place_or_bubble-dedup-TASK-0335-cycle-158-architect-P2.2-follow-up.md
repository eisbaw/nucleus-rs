### Gotchas & subtleties (forward-carried lessons)

- **Tile-key safety from cross-pass tile-tracking parallel.** The 4→5 tuple widening at place_or_bubble is safe because per-Op tile accumulation and hoist tile accumulation are structurally identical — neither tracker can deviate without the other deviating. This is a NON-LOCAL invariant; the docstring at the closure now explicitly enumerates the three structural facts that carry it (per-Op + hoist parallel construction; drain rewrite at line 1186; closure rewrite at line ~1535). A future refactor that decouples per-Op tile-tracking from hoist tile-tracking would break this property silently.
- **'Redundant-by-construction' is a checkable claim.** Cycle-159 architect P2.1 caught the initial docstring's false claim with a 5-minute structural audit. The recurrence pattern `feedback-comment-doc-lie-recurring` fires again.
- **Append-at-tail vs insert-at-slot is a load-bearing distinction.** Cycle-158's helper is for the former; the slot-aware sibling is for the latter. Future dedup-site additions in transfer_inject must pick the correct shape; the wrong choice silently over-suppresses.
