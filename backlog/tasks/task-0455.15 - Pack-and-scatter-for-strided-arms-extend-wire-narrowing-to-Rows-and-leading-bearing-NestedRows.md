## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
TASK-0453.22 narrows only contiguous spans (RecvBasis::Flat; NestedRows with empty leading). The strided arms stay whole-array: Rows (blocks2d, e.g. 07-matmul/distributed-2d c; 05-stencil/distributed-2d halo) and leading-bearing NestedRows (16-jacobi field cumulative halo). Extending narrowing there needs pack-and-scatter: gather the strided sub-rows into a contiguous send buffer on the producer and a packed-source row-loop receiver paste — a NEW receiver paste shape, not just an offset change.

CAUTION (from the wave-5 trap map): 16-jacobi field is cumulative cross-iteration state — the xN-double-count history; any change to its transfer shape must re-verify the banded COPY semantics. The blocks2d dual-Push-class (host main + halo strip) must keep distinct seqs.

Wire savings upside: the strided cells are exactly the 2D-partitioned ones where whole-array overhead grows worst with worker count.
