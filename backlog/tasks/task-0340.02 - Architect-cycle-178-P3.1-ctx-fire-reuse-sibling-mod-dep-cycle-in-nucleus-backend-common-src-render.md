## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Architect cycle-178 P3 finding (low priority — Rust permits sibling-module cycles, but it's a smell signal that the seam choice could be improved). After the cycle-178 render.rs split: fire.rs imports sidecar_consts_to_resolved + try_reuse_axis_offset from reuse.rs, while reuse.rs imports data_name + render_flat_index from fire.rs, AND ctx.rs imports ReuseRewriteGroup from reuse.rs. Three of the six sub-modules participate in the cycle.

Options:
1. Move ReuseRewriteGroup to a leaf module (e.g. render/group.rs) so the dep graph becomes a DAG; ctx.rs and fire.rs both depend on group.rs but not on each other or reuse.rs.
2. Move the two pub(super) helpers (sidecar_consts_to_resolved, try_reuse_axis_offset) into fire.rs (they're Fire-arg rewriting concerns); reuse.rs then only consumes from fire.rs, not the other way around. Risk: this moves the cross-module helpers AWAY from their per-affine-decomposition logic.
3. Accept the cycle (Rust permits it; the pub(super) widening is minimal).

Not blocking; file for a future hygiene cycle.
